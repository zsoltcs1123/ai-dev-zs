# Migrate 1.3 to 1.4: unified source pages

## Preconditions

`scaffold` is `1.3`.

## Steps

1. Tier A additive sync per [migrate.md](../commands/migrate.md) when the dry-run lists missing scaffold paths.
2. For each `wiki/sources/{author}/quick/*.md` (`slug` = basename without `.md`, `main` = `wiki/sources/{author}/{slug}.md`):
   - Strip YAML from quick and main before merging bodies.
   - **`main` exists:** Split trailing `## Citations` off `main`. Hoist summarize `#`, `Source:`, and `Author:` once after frontmatter; `## Quick summary` + quick body (no title, source, or author); `## Summary` + full body without title, source, author, or citations; citations block unchanged, or `## Citations` / `No citations found.` if absent.
   - **No `main`:** Create `wiki/sources/{author}/{slug}.md` with quick frontmatter (if any), quick `#` as page title when present, quick body under `## Quick summary`, and `## Citations` / `No citations found.` until ingest fills full summary and citations from raw.
   - Delete the quick file.
3. Remove empty `wiki/sources/{author}/quick/` directories.
4. Optional: where `raw/sources/` or `raw/notes/` still has capture, run `citations` on raw and replace the page’s citations section.
5. Set `scaffold` to `1.4` in `.wiki-root.yaml`.

Layout: [source-page.md](../source-page.md).

## Verification

- No `wiki/sources/**/quick/*.md` (unless the user skipped a wiki).
- Source pages match [source-page.md](../source-page.md); citations last.
- `wiki/index.md` and raw capture unchanged.
