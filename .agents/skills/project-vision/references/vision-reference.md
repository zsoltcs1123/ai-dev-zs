# VISION.md Reference

Section reference and quality bar for the VISION.md produced by the project-vision skill.

## Section Reference

Title: `# [Project Name] — Vision`. Use `---` between sections. Required ●, optional ○.

| #   | Section              | Req | Include When                           | Format                                                           |
| --- | -------------------- | --- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Executive Summary    | ○   | Enterprise; stakeholders need overview | 1-2 paragraphs                                                   |
| 2   | Problem Statement    | ●   | Always                                 | Who / current state / pain points                                |
| 3   | Users & Stakeholders | ○   | Multiple roles or decision-makers      | `\| Role \| Needs \| Notes \|`                                   |
| 4   | Vision & Success     | ●   | Always                                 | `**Vision:**` → `**Success looks like:**` → `**Metrics:**` table |
| 5   | Scope                | ●   | Always                                 | In Scope / Out of Scope / Future subsections                     |
| 6   | Constraints          | ○   | Tech/business/design limits exist      | Technical / Business / Design subsections                        |
| 7   | Assumptions          | ○   | Unvalidated beliefs driving decisions  | `\| Assumption \| Validation Needed \|`                          |
| 8   | Dependencies         | ○   | External systems/services required     | `\| Dependency \| Version \| Risk \|`                            |
| 9   | Risks & Mitigations  | ○   | High-stakes; known unknowns            | `\| Risk \| Likelihood \| Impact \| Mitigation \|`               |
| 10  | Version History      | ●   | Always                                 | `\| Version \| Date \| Changes \|`                               |

## Quality Bar

Every statement must be specific and validated. Scale the document to the project — a weekend project needs half a page; an enterprise system may need 5+.
