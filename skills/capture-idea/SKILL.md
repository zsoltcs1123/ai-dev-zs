# Capture Idea

## Outcome

Concise bullet points that capture the essence of an idea from freeform input — keywords, fragments, brain dump, or conversation excerpt. Raw fragments are fine. Default output in chat; write to file only when asked.

## Guardrails

- Record only. Do not explore feasibility, tech choices, structure, or next steps unless explicitly asked.
- Absorb and distill; do not interrogate.
- Reduce long conversations to essentials; do not transcribe.
